Beyond the nine changes

What the nine leave open

  • Whether an assertion may retry once is recorded rather than decided, and left open one flaky test closes fourteen gates instead of one.
  • Scoping a commit to its own paths can leave a path the step edited uncommitted while the step’s marker still lands in history.
  • Commit scoping fixes the measured case only: another session’s edit made during a step’s hours is still swept into that step’s commit.
  • Two of the nine change the workflow emitter, so the byte-for-byte golden workflows are regenerated twice and the second diff hides surprises.
  • Tests assert the documents’ contents, so docs/run-model.md has room to name exactly one more exclusion cause across all nine changes.
