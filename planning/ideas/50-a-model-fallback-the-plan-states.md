# 50 — A model fallback the plan states

**Status: idea.** Section E of [45](../archive/45-spending-the-allowance-knowingly.md), the open
question 45 carried and did not build.

**North star ([principle 4](../../PRINCIPLES.md), [principle 3](../../PRINCIPLES.md)).** A long
hold at the allowance has three answers: wait, switch to a cheaper model, or stop. Cairn builds
only the first. The person should be able to choose another in advance, in the plan, rather than
learn of the hold from a stalled queue.

## The idea

A plan sentence such as _if the weekly Opus allowance is low, use sonnet_ would let the derivation
record a fallback for each step. Admission would then take the fallback rather than hold. Per
[SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md), a model is never chosen on the person's behalf, so a fallback exists
only where the plan states one.

## Open questions

1. **Whether to build it at all.** A step that ran on a fallback did different work from the step
   the plan priced. The record would have to say which model ran, and the report would have to
   say that the fallback was taken.
2. **What "low" means.** The hold threshold, the pacing 45 C added, or a level the plan states.
